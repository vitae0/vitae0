## Hello

I am an independent student researcher interested in mathematical analysis, number theory, and computational methods. My work combines theoretical investigation with exact computation, symbolic experimentation, and software development.

I maintain research notes, proof attempts, computational tools, and independent software projects. Mathematical claims are distinguished from conjectures and numerical observations.
